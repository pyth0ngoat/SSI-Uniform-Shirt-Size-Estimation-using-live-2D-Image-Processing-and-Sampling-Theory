Due to insufficient sample size, a predictive model for the GIRLS uniform category could not be fitted. The pre-specified statistical gating protocol requires a minimum of 40 accepted participants overall, with at least 10 to 15 participants per modeled size class to ensure reliable cross-validation and generalization. Because only 26 girls provided valid data and their distribution was heavily skewed, merging adjacent size bands ultimately collapsed the dataset into a single target class, leaving no valid classification target. Consequently, we rely solely on descriptive statistics to characterize this subgroup.
